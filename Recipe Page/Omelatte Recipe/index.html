Simple Omelatte Recipe

An easy and quick dish, Perfect for any meal. This classic Omelatte combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, vegetables, cooked meats

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In a bowl, mix eggs with salt and pepper until combined.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick pan on medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the eggs: Pour in the eggs and tilt the pan so they spread evenly.
  4. Add fillings: When the edges start to set, add your chosen fillings.
  5. Fold the omelette: Lift one side and fold it over the fillings.
  6. Serve hot: Cook for another minute and transfer to a plate.
